Show HN: NYC Survive – Times Square has gone dark. Keep them alive.

I wanted a survival sim where nothing happens unless I decide it. No idle timers, no autoplay. NYC Survive drops you in Times Square after the lights go out. You place shelters, assign work, ration food and hold the line at night. Every loss is a decision you made. Web at nyc.heyitsmejosh.com, source on GitHub.
