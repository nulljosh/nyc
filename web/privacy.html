Privacy Policy

NYC Survive · Last updated 3 August 2026

NYC Survive does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

Game data

Your saved games and settings are stored locally on your own device so the app can restore your progress. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network access

The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else. We operate no servers that receive information from the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any change that involves collecting data would also require a new app release, reviewed by Apple, before it could take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: trommatic@icloud.com
